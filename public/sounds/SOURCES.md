| clay-select.wav | Audio/select_002.ogg | Choose a work | 90% playback speed, 2.6 kHz low-pass, 2 ms / 12 ms fades, −24.9 dBFS RMS (peak limited) |
| paper-open.wav | Audio/open_001.ogg | Open a case or follow a navigation link | 86% playback speed, 4.2 kHz low-pass, short fades, −25 dBFS RMS |

All derivatives are mono 44.1 kHz 16-bit PCM WAV for browser compatibility.
The player applies a further 0.65 master gain. No hovering, scrolling, or background music triggers audio.
